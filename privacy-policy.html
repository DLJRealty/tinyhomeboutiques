Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DLJ Properties ("we," "us") handles information when you visit tinyhomeboutiques.com or contact us about a stay.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us — such as your name, email, phone number, and message — when you make a booking inquiry or contact us. We also collect standard, non-identifying analytics data (pages visited, device type, general location) through Google Analytics to understand how the site is used.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your inquiries, process and support your booking, and improve our website and guest experience. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Third parties

When you book through a platform such as Airbnb or VRBO, your information is handled under that platform's own privacy policy. We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to measure site traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time.

Your choices

You may ask us to access or delete the personal information you have shared with us directly. Email DLJrealty@yahoo.com and we will respond promptly.
