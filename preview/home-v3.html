Questions

Before you pack.

Where are the homes?

All six homes are in Woodland Park, Colorado (80863) — in the mountains about 30 minutes from Colorado Springs, near Garden of the Gods, Cheyenne Mountain Zoo, and the Pikes Peak region's trails.

How do I book?

Directly through our secure booking engine — it shows live availability for every home. Each home is also listed on Airbnb, where you can read all of our guest reviews.

Can I bring my pet?

To maintain the pristine, allergen-free environment our guests love, our homes are pet-free. If you're traveling with pets, message us before booking and we'll try to point you in the right direction.

Do the homes really have Pikes Peak views?

Our Boutique TinyHome has a direct view of Pikes Peak, and several homes have panoramic mountain views — many guests say waking up to the mountain is the highlight of their stay.
